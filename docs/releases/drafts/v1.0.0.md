## Release summary

WealthWise 1.0 is an offline-first personal finance app for tracking
transactions, budgets, investments, liabilities, savings goals, insurance and
scheduled dues. It adds recurring Budget income, local reminders, encrypted
backup options and first-release accessibility, migration and reliability
hardening.

## New features

- **Budget income recurrence:** plan income monthly, quarterly, half-yearly or
  yearly, set the first due month for non-monthly income, and review due items
  and projected cash flow. Existing month snapshots retain their saved values.
- **Investments:** track manual and recurring holdings, record deposits and
  withdrawals, review investment entries, and use supported valuation and
  return calculations. Recurring plans can be stopped at an inclusive date and
  resumed without rewriting investment history.
- **Liabilities:** track payment schedules and EMI payments, and close eligible
  recurring liabilities early with the related settlement and future-dues
  behavior.
- **Goals and Emergency Fund:** connect eligible holdings to savings goals and
  Emergency Fund allocations, with progress and allocation views.
- **Insurance:** manage policies and scheduled premium dues.
- **Local reminders:** opt in to privacy-aware on-device payment reminders
  through the in-app prompt.
- **Backup and restore:** validate and restore local backups; optionally
  encrypt exported backups with a passphrase.

## Improvements

- Home, Transactions, Goals and Budget provide connected views of balances,
  financial activity, goals and scheduled dues.
- Recurring income contributes only in its due months across Budget totals,
  Received dues and projections; existing saved month snapshots are preserved.
- Investment and liability schedule changes flow through shared projections
  and support Undo where implemented.
- Accessibility labels, touch-target and large-text behavior have regression
  coverage. Long-history fixtures and performance checks protect key
  calculation and rendering paths; no physical-device timing claim is made.

## Security and privacy

- Financial data is stored locally; this release has no cloud account or
  runtime synchronization.
- Encrypted backup export protects the exported file only. App data stored on
  the device is not encrypted by this feature, and a forgotten backup
  passphrase cannot be recovered.
- The current production audit reports 7 advisories (4 moderate, 3 high).
  Patch-level fixes compatible with Expo SDK 57 were unavailable at review;
  major Expo/Router upgrades require a separate owner decision.

## Compatibility and migration

The persisted schema is v22. On upgrade from schema v21, existing Budget
income items are assigned monthly frequency. No manual data migration is
required. Backup import retains support for historical schemas covered by the
existing migration and compatibility tests.

## Known limitations

- There is no automatic bank synchronization or live market-price feed;
  financial records and manually maintained values depend on user input.
- On-device data is not encrypted at rest by backup encryption.
- Financial calculations and projections depend on recorded information and
  documented calculation assumptions; they are not professional financial,
  tax, legal or insurance advice.
- Native-device layout, accessibility and performance behavior should be
  confirmed on target iOS and Android devices.

## Upgrade instructions

Install the approved v1.0.0 build through its distribution channel. Existing
schema-v21 data migrates automatically to v22. Before replacing or erasing
important data, export and retain a backup. Keep the passphrase for an
encrypted backup separately; it cannot be recovered.
