# WealthWise Community Guidelines and Code of Conduct

WealthWise is an open, offline-first personal finance application. We welcome contributors, users, and community members from all backgrounds and experience levels. Our goal is to foster a productive, respectful, and safe collaborative environment.

## Our Standards

We encourage behaviors that contribute to a healthy and inclusive community:

- **Respect and Professionalism:** Communicate with courtesy, patience, and mutual respect. Value differing perspectives and constructive discourse.
- **Constructive Collaboration:** Focus on what is best for the project and community. Provide helpful, thoughtful feedback on code, design, and documentation.
- **Privacy and Data Stewardship:** WealthWise handles sensitive financial concepts. Never post, request, or expose real personal financial records, credentials, account numbers, or backup archives in public discussions, issues, or pull requests.
- **Empathy and Inclusion:** Be welcoming to newcomers and assist others in learning project conventions, test practices, and offline-first architectural patterns.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are considered harassment and are strictly prohibited:

- Offensive comments, insults, derogatory language, or personal attacks.
- Harassment in any form, public or private.
- Publishing others' private information, such as real financial figures, physical or email addresses, without explicit permission.
- Trolling, disruptive commentary, or spamming project discussions.
- Any conduct that reasonably compromises the safety, well-being, or dignity of community members.

## Responsibilities and Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and upholding our standards of acceptable behavior. Maintainers will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that violates these guidelines.

Maintainers have the authority to edit, reject, or remove comments, issues, pull requests, and other contributions that do not align with these guidelines, and may temporarily or permanently suspend any participant whose conduct is unacceptable.

## Reporting Violations

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it privately to the project maintainers through private GitHub channels (such as repository security advisories or maintainer contact links).

All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with confidentiality and care.

## Scope

These guidelines apply within all official project spaces—including issues, pull requests, discussions, code reviews, and related community channels—as well as when an individual is representing the project in public forums.
